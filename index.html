Welcome to SSIM 2026

The 6th International Conference on Social Sciences and Intelligence Management (SSIM 2026)

December 17–19, 2026, Chaoyang University of Technology, Taichung, Taiwan

Theme: Human-Centered AI, Sustainable Digital Ecosystems, and Intelligent Transformation in Social Sciences

Welcome to the 6th International Conference on Social Sciences and Intelligence Management (SSIM 2026), an international interdisciplinary forum dedicated to advancing research, innovation, and collaboration at the intersection of artificial intelligence, digital transformation, and the social sciences.

Hosted by Chaoyang University of Technology, Taiwan, SSIM 2026 will bring together researchers, educators, policymakers, industry professionals, and technology innovators from around the world to explore how intelligent technologies can be designed, governed, and applied to create sustainable, inclusive, and human-centered futures.

As artificial intelligence increasingly shapes education, communication, healthcare, governance, business, and everyday life, the need for responsible innovation and ethical digital transformation has never been greater. SSIM 2026 provides a platform for meaningful dialogue on the opportunities and challenges of emerging technologies while emphasizing their impact on individuals, communities, and society.

The conference welcomes original research contributions, practical applications, theoretical developments, and interdisciplinary perspectives in areas including:

  • Human-Centered Artificial Intelligence
  • Sustainable Digital Ecosystems
  • Computational Social Sciences
  • Intelligent Learning and Educational Technologies
  • Digital Humanities and Communication
  • AI Governance and Responsible Innovation
  • Intelligent Healthcare and Social Services
  • Business Analytics and Sustainable Governance
  • Advanced Data Analytics and Machine Learning Applications

Building upon the successful achievements of previous SSIM conferences and their internationally indexed proceedings, SSIM 2026 continues its commitment to academic excellence, rigorous peer review, and global research engagement. The conference will be conducted in a hybrid format, enabling both onsite and online participation to foster worldwide collaboration and knowledge exchange.

We warmly invite scholars, researchers, practitioners, and students to join us in Taichung, Taiwan, from December 17–19, 2026, to share innovative ideas, establish international partnerships, and contribute to shaping the future of intelligent and sustainable societies.

We look forward to welcoming you to SSIM 2026.

Together, we explore how human values and intelligent technologies can co-create a more sustainable, inclusive, and innovative world.
