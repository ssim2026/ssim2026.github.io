Publication

SSIM 2026 is committed to maintaining high academic standards and ensuring wide international visibility of its conference outputs. Accepted and presented papers will be published in peer-reviewed conference proceedings and submitted for indexing in major scholarly databases.

Conference Proceedings

The SSIM 2026 conference proceedings are currently planned to be published through Springer, with a focus on the Humanities and Social Sciences series. Depending on the final thematic alignment and paper submissions, a joint publication arrangement with an Information and Computer Science / Intelligent Systems series may also be considered, similar to the SSIM 2025 publication model.

All submissions will undergo a rigorous peer-review process to ensure academic quality, originality, and relevance.

Springer Publication Information

Springer Publication Information

  • Springer Nature Conference Proceedings
  • Humanities and Social Sciences Series (primary track)
  • Potential joint inclusion with an Information/Computer Science series (subject to final approval)

Final confirmation of the publication series will be announced after the paper acceptance and thematic classification process.

Indexing Information

The published proceedings will be submitted for evaluation and indexing in internationally recognized databases, including:

  • Scopus
  • EI Compendex (Engineering Village) (subject to Springer indexing approval)
  • Other relevant academic databases depending on publication classification

Indexing outcomes are determined independently by the indexing services and cannot be guaranteed by the conference.

SSIM 2021–2025 Archive

Since its establishment, the International Conference on Social Sciences and Intelligence Management (SSIM) has grown into an internationally recognized academic platform that promotes interdisciplinary research and global collaboration in social sciences, education, management, and intelligent technologies. Over the past five editions, SSIM has successfully hosted hundreds of paper presentations and attracted scholars, researchers, educators, practitioners, and industry experts from around the world.

The SSIM Archive preserves the history and achievements of previous conferences, including conference themes, keynote speeches, technical programs, organizing committees, proceedings publications, and conference highlights. These archives demonstrate SSIM’s continuous commitment to academic excellence, international engagement, interdisciplinary innovation, and research impact.
