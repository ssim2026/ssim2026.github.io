About SSIM

The International Conference on Social Sciences and Intelligence Management (SSIM) is an international academic conference dedicated to fostering interdisciplinary dialogue and collaboration among researchers, educators, professionals, policymakers, and industry experts. SSIM serves as a global platform for presenting innovative research, exchanging ideas, and exploring emerging developments in social sciences, education, management, communication, digital technologies, and artificial intelligence.

As the world continues to experience rapid technological and societal transformation, SSIM aims to promote meaningful discussions on how intelligent technologies can be applied to address complex social challenges while supporting sustainable development and human well-being. The conference encourages the integration of diverse disciplinary perspectives and welcomes contributions that bridge theory, research, and practical applications.

SSIM 2026 continues this mission by bringing together international scholars and practitioners to explore the conference theme: “Human-Centered AI, Sustainable Digital Ecosystems, and Intelligent Transformation in Social Sciences.”

Conference History

Since its establishment in 2021, the International Conference on Social Sciences and Intelligence Management (SSIM) has grown into a recognized international forum for scholarly exchange and interdisciplinary collaboration. Over the past five years, SSIM has successfully attracted participants from numerous countries and regions, providing opportunities for researchers and professionals to share innovative ideas, present research findings, and establish international academic networks.

Despite the challenges posed by the global pandemic during its early years, SSIM successfully adopted flexible conference formats, including virtual and hybrid participation, ensuring continuous international engagement and academic exchange. Each edition of the conference has featured a diverse range of topics spanning social sciences, education, management, information technology, digital innovation, and emerging intelligent systems.

The conference has consistently attracted a substantial number of paper submissions and presentations from universities, research institutions, government agencies, and industry sectors worldwide. Through its commitment to academic quality, internationalization, and interdisciplinary collaboration, SSIM has established itself as a valuable platform for discussing contemporary issues and future trends in social sciences and intelligent management.

In 2026, SSIM proudly celebrates its 6th edition, continuing its tradition of academic excellence and international cooperation. The conference remains committed to creating opportunities for meaningful dialogue, knowledge exchange, and collaborative research that contribute to both scholarly advancement and societal development.
