The 6th International Conference on Social Sciences and Intelligence Management (SSIM 2026)

Human-Centered AI, Sustainable Digital Ecosystems, and Intelligent Transformation in Social Sciences

December 17–19, 2026

Chaoyang University of Technology, Taiwan

Email: cyut.ssim2026pcs@gmail.com

SSIM 2026 serves as a premier interdisciplinary forum focusing on human-centered artificial intelligence, intelligent digital ecosystems, computational transformation, and responsible innovation across education, communication, governance, healthcare, and social systems.

Building upon the successful outcomes of previous SSIM iterations, the 2026 conference emphasizes the strategic integration of intelligent technologies with sustainable social development, ethical governance, and data-driven innovation in the humanities and social sciences.

The 2026 conference aims to foster a dynamic global environment for researchers, educators, industry practitioners, policymakers, and technology developers to exchange emerging findings, advanced computational methodologies, cross-disciplinary applications, and practical innovations. We welcome empirical, theoretical, methodological, and applied research contributions.

Special emphasis will be placed on:

  • Human-centered AI and ethical intelligence system
  • Sustainable digital ecosystems
  • AI governance and responsible innovation
  • Computational social sciences
  • Data-driven educational technologies
  • Digital humanities and intelligent communication
  • Cross-disciplinary intelligent transformation frameworks
